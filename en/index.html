Talk to us before you sign

Most tax problems don't arise where someone is unsure — they arise where someone feels certain and therefore doesn't ask. In the contract that's already signed. In the property that's already sitting in the wrong entity.

We'd rather have the conversation beforehand than the repair afterward. And before a conversation turns into a billable engagement, we'll tell you so.

Knowledge is everywhere. The right question isn't.

Tax knowledge has never been hard to find — and not just since AI. Anyone who searches will find an answer.

The trouble is, a wrongly framed question gets a very convincing wrong answer. Our work starts before that: recognising the real issue behind your question, asking the right question, and standing behind the answer.

What we stand for

Before, not after

We're present in day-to-day business, not just at year-end close. Better one coffee too many than one message too late.

Close enough to push back

We're close to your business — which is exactly why we'll tell you what you don't want to hear. We'll argue with you if we believe there's a better outcome.

An answer, not an essay

You get an assessment you can actually decide on — not a ten-page report that ends with "it depends."

Sound, not maximal

We're not chasing the lowest possible tax bill — we're after decisions that still hold up in four years.
