Most tax problems don't start where someone is unsure. They start where someone feels certain, and therefore doesn't ask.

In the contract that's already signed. In the property that's already sitting in the wrong entity. We'd rather have the conversation beforehand than the repair afterward, and before a conversation turns into a billable engagement, we'll tell you so. Tax knowledge has never been hard to find, not just since AI. The trouble is, a wrongly framed question gets a very convincing wrong answer. Our work begins before that.

What we stand for

Before, not after

We're present in day-to-day business, not just at the year-end close. Better one coffee too many than one message too late.

Close enough to push back

We're close to your business, and that's exactly why we'll also tell you what you don't want to hear. We'll argue with you if we believe there's a better outcome.

An answer, not an essay

You get an assessment you can actually decide on. Not a ten-page report that ends with "it depends."

Sound, not maximal

We're not chasing the lowest possible tax bill, we're after decisions that still hold up in four years.
