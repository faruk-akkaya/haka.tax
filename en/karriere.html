Around 30 employees, four partners at eye level, and always an open ear for new people who enjoy tax law.

What to expect with us

No empty phrases — just the things that genuinely matter to us day to day.

Digital processes. Receipts, reports and communication all run digitally. Paper files are the exception with us.

Workspaces worth the name. Electric height-adjustable desks, 34-inch curved monitors plus a vertical second display. Paperless isn't a goal for us — it's the default.

Mobile working. Part of your working time can be done flexibly from home or on the go.

Fixed points of contact. You work with real clients and real responsibility, not anonymous processing.

Ongoing training as standard. We actively support further training, professional exams and specialisations.

Industry focus instead of one-size-fits-all. IT, real estate, logistics, manufacturing and intangible assets. It's never boring here.

Fair, transparent pay. Performance and development are noticeably reflected in salary, regardless of gender.
