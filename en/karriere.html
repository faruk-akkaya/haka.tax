What to expect working with us

No empty phrases, just the things that actually matter to us day to day.

Digital processes. Receipts, reports and communication run digitally. Paper files are the exception here.

Workspaces that deserve the name. Electric height-adjustable desks, 34-inch curved monitors plus a vertical second screen. Paperless isn't a goal for us, it's the default.

Remote work. Part of your working time can be done flexibly from home or on the go.

Fixed points of contact. You work with real clients and real responsibility, not anonymous processing.

Continuing education, as a matter of course. We actively support further training, professional exams and specialisations.

Industry focus instead of one-size-fits-all. IT, real estate, logistics, manufacturing and intangible assets. It never gets boring here.

Fair, transparent pay. Performance and development are reflected noticeably in your pay, regardless of gender.
