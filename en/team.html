Of Counsel

WP/StB Gerhard Gsänger

What began as a dedicated sole practice, Mr. Gsänger successfully grew into an established partnership (formerly Gsänger & Hechtel Wirtschaftsprüfer Steuerberater) by later bringing on a partner. By handing over his shares in a spirit of trust, he laid the strong foundation for today's HAKA Partnerschaft mbB. We consider ourselves fortunate that, after this generational transition, he continues to support us and our clients with his valuable experience.

The Feel-Good Manager

Marvel, aka "Marvelito"

Our handsome male Galgo, Marvel, enriches our firm as a dedicated Feel-Good Manager who prefers to announce his enthusiasm with a joyful "woo-woo-woo." With his unerring sense for break times, he charmingly reminds us to breathe during the working day and keeps us from overtime madness. As a true professional, he pays meticulous attention to his work-life balance and firmly insists on regular snacks as well as his contractually guaranteed walk times. Thanks to his deep love of people, he creates a relaxed, warm atmosphere that perfectly underlines our modern team spirit. In his free time, he trades the desk for the outdoors. Our office hero finally spends a well-earned evening on the sofa at home.
