Of Counsel

WP/StB Gerhard Gsänger

What began as a dedicated sole practice, Mr. Gsänger successfully grew into an established partnership (formerly Gsänger & Hechtel Wirtschaftsprüfer Steuerberater) by later taking on a partner. By entrusting the handover of his shares, he laid the strong foundation for today's HAKA Partnerschaft mbB. We consider ourselves fortunate that, after this generational change, he continues to support us and our clients with his valuable experience.

The Feel-Good Manager

Marvel, aka “Marvelito”

Our stately Galgo Marvel enriches our firm as a dedicated Feel-Good Manager, who prefers to announce his motivation with a joyful “Wuwuwuh.” With his unfailing instinct for proper break times, he charmingly reminds us to catch our breath during the working day, and protects us from overtime madness. As a true professional, he pays meticulous attention to his work-life balance and firmly insists on regular snacks as well as his contractually guaranteed walk times. Thanks to his unconditional love of people, he creates a relaxed, warm atmosphere that perfectly underlines our modern team spirit. In his free time, he trades the desk for the outdoors. Our firm's hero finally spends his well-earned evenings on the living-room sofa at home.
