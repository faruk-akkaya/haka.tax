Tax advice
that understands your industry

From day-to-day bookkeeping to the most complex valuation question.

Ongoing support

The reliable foundation for every engagement: digital, on time, and free of surprises.

Financial accounting. Digital receipt capture, ongoing bookkeeping and real-time reporting instead of once a quarter.

Payroll. Reliable monthly payroll, including all reporting and contribution obligations.

Annual financial statements. Financial statements and profit determinations from a single source, from sole proprietorships to large corporations.

Tax returns. We prepare all business and personal tax returns, as well as your e-balance sheet.

Tax audit support. We support you through tax authority audits: prepared, documented, and on equal footing.

Tax structuring advice. Restructuring, buying and selling businesses, due diligence and succession, supported from the first idea through to completion.

Business advisory. Forward-looking structuring instead of pure hindsight, for investments, financing and growth.

Legal advice. We can do a lot, but for legal matters we rely on our partner law firm. We're happy to make a personal introduction.

Audit experience that feeds into our advice

We are not an audit firm and don't accept audit engagements. Two of our partners are qualified auditors (Wirtschaftsprüfer), and we put that knowledge to work where it benefits you most: whether a given structure would hold up under audit.

If you need an audit, for example a statutory audit or one in connection with a transaction, we make the introduction to a partner firm and continue to support you on the tax side.

Intangible Assets

Brands, patents, software and licences can't be valued by a fixed formula. We advise on accounting treatment, transfer pricing and the clean transfer of intangible assets.

Real Estate

Whether long-term holder, project developer or family office: we advise on real estate transfer tax, depreciation, share deals and a structure that will hold up to a later audit.

Corporation or Partnership

Every legal form comes with its own obligations and room for structuring. We tailor our advice accordingly.

Corporations

GmbH, UG, GmbH & Co. KG or AG: accounting under the German Commercial Code (HGB), constructive dividends, shareholder-director questions, and structuring around distributions or retained earnings.

Partnerships

GbR, OHG or KG: profit determination by cash accounting or balance sheet, special business assets, partner changes, and the pitfalls of succession.
