4. Contact form

If you send us enquiries via the contact form, your details from the form, including the contact data you provide there, are stored by us for the purpose of processing the enquiry and in case of follow-up questions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to enquiries).

5. Client-engagement-related data processing

As part of our tax advisory services, we process personal and financial data of our clients to the extent necessary to fulfil the engagement. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR in conjunction with the professional retention obligations (including Section 66 StBerG, Section 147 AO).

6. Cookies

Except where technically essential, this website does not use cookies for analytics or marketing purposes without your prior consent.

7. Disclosure of data

Your data is only transferred to third parties where legally required, where you have given consent, or where necessary to carry out the engagement (for example, to tax authorities as part of handling your case).

8. Retention period

Personal data from contact enquiries is deleted as soon as it is no longer required for processing. Client-engagement documents are subject to statutory retention periods (generally 10 years pursuant to Section 147 AO / Section 66 StBerG).

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to the processing of your personal data. Please contact us at the address given above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority — in Bavaria: the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA).

10. Changes to this privacy policy

We update this privacy policy whenever changes to our data processing make it necessary. The version published on this page at any given time applies.
